Harmony, Inc.

An international organization of women singing barbershop harmony, founded in 1959. Its choruses and quartets across North America compete, perform and teach — and High Gear is proud to be one of them.

Barbershop Harmony Society

Founded in 1938 in Tulsa, Oklahoma, and now headquartered in Nashville, the Barbershop Harmony Society kept the barbershop tradition alive through the twentieth century. Today it welcomes singers of every gender in quartets and choruses across North America and beyond.

High Gear competes in the Northeast District.
